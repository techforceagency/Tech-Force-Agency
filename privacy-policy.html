Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our website (https://techforceagency.com/) or request a consultation, we collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, business email address, company name, phone number, and service requirements submitted via our contact forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information solely to evaluate your business operational requirements, match you with qualified remote specialists, respond to inquiries, process service agreements, and communicate regarding active service engagements.

3. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Tech Force Agency enforces strict data confidentiality. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business information to third parties. All client operational data and workflows are protected under mutual Non-Disclosure Agreements (NDAs).

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption, access controls, and secure password vault protocols (e.g., 1Password, LastPass) to prevent unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse of your business data.
