Email Marketing & Automation Capabilities

1. Broadcast Campaign Setup

Format, QA, test, and schedule weekly newsletters, promotional blasts, and product announcements.

  • • Cross-client rendering checks (Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail)
  • • Link testing and UTM campaign tracking validation
  • • Time-zone send optimization (EST, CST, PST)

Configure behavioral trigger sequences that convert and retain subscribers on autopilot.

  • • Post-purchase review requests & replenishment reminders
  • • Re-engagement and win-back automation

Segment your subscriber database to maximize relevance and maintain healthy inbox placement.

  • • Suppressing inactive subscribers & hard bounces

  • • CAN-SPAM and GDPR/CCPA compliance management

Execute structured split tests to discover winning subject lines, send times, and call-to-actions.

  • • Subject line & preview text split testing
  • • Send-time optimization experiments
  • • CTA button layout & copy comparisons
  • • Documenting test outcomes in campaign logs

6. Performance Reporting

Compile clean weekly and monthly performance dashboards for stakeholder review.
