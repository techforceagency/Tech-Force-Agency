Core Social Media Execution Tasks

We focus strictly on the operational implementation of your social media strategy—keeping your profiles consistent and engaged.

1. Content Calendar Scheduling

Organize approved copy, imagery, and video assets into an orderly weekly/monthly calendar queue.

  • • Loading posts into scheduling software
  • • Optimizing post timing for peak audience reach
  • • Verifying link previews and UTM tracking parameters
  • • Maintaining editorial consistency across profiles

2. Multi-Platform Publishing

Execute timely daily posts formatted specifically for each social network's technical requirements.

  • • LinkedIn company page & executive profile posting
  • • Instagram feed posts, carousels, and stories
  • • X (Twitter) threads and timely industry updates
  • • Facebook business page announcements

3. Community Triage & Moderation

Ensure your audience feels heard while protecting your brand reputation from spam and negative bots.

  • • Responding promptly to inbound comments and questions
  • • Triaging Direct Messages (DMs) to sales or customer support
  • • Removing spam links and offensive commentary
  • • Liking and acknowledging user mentions and shares

4. Asset & Media Organization

Keep your creative repository structured and accessible so content production never stalls.

  • • Organizing Google Drive, Dropbox, and Canva folders
  • • Resizing images for standard social dimensions
  • • Archiving past campaign graphics and videos
  • • Curating approved brand hashtag sets

5. Account Hygiene & Updates

Keep your profile bios, banner images, link-in-bio tools, and featured links fresh and accurate.

  • • Updating Linktree, Bento, or website bio links
  • • Refreshing profile banners during webinars or events
  • • Auditing business contact details across platforms
  • • Pinning timely announcements and top-performing posts

6. Performance & Engagement Logging

Compile clear monthly engagement reports so your leadership team understands top-performing content.

  • • Logging impression, reach, and engagement totals
  • • Tracking follower growth trends
  • • Identifying high-performing content formats
  • • Providing raw metrics for leadership review
